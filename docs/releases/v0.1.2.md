# MaxRAR v0.1.2

**Signed installers, a Windows 11 main-menu entry, and the experimental MRAR/2 format.**

## Changes

- **Signed apps.** The Windows installer and app are signed with Azure Artifact Signing. The Mac apps are signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. First-launch security warnings should be fewer or gone; Windows SmartScreen may still warn until the signature builds reputation.
- **Windows 11 main right-click menu.** A **MaxRAR** submenu with **Open in MaxRAR**, **Extract with MaxRAR…** and **Compress with MaxRAR…** now appears without **Show more options**. Compress works with several selected items. The classic commands stay available as a fallback and on Windows 10.
- **MRAR/2 (experimental).** New `.mrar` archives compress files together in 16 MiB blocks and pick PPMd, LZMA2 or no compression for each block. In our tests on one Windows PC, text archives were 20% smaller than 7-Zip -mx9 and about 38× faster to create; a real `node_modules` folder was still 6% larger than 7-Zip. Archives are written and checked on several threads. MRAR/0 and MRAR/1 archives still open. See the [MRAR/2 specification](https://github.com/MatilyLabs/MaxRAR/blob/main/docs/MRAR-2-SPEC.md).
- Mac installers are named for the chip: `MaxRAR_0.1.2_macOS_apple-silicon.dmg` and `MaxRAR_0.1.2_macOS_intel.dmg`.

## Installing

Existing v0.1.1 installations can update from **About MaxRAR**. Otherwise choose the Windows installer, the Mac DMG for your chip, or a Linux package from this release. The `darwin-*`, `windows-*` and `linux-*` files are for the in-app updater; you do not need to download them.

MRAR remains experimental: keep your originals and use established formats for exchange and backups. Older MaxRAR versions cannot open MRAR/2 archives. RAR creation, multipart RAR and self-extracting RAR are not supported. Scan remains limited local inspection, not comprehensive antivirus protection.
